# Persistence Governance Decision and Migration Readiness Review

Date: 2026-07-25
Status: D.6 `CONDITIONALLY_READY_FOR_CONTROLLED_MIGRATION_REHEARSAL`
Scope: Governance decision only; no migration execution

## 1. Purpose

D.6 converts D.4c through D.5d persistence evidence into a formal governance
decision. It MUST decide the next allowed activity and MUST NOT execute
migration, indexing, retrieval activation, parser processing, OCR, dependency
remediation, or production persistence.

## 2. Scope

This review covers readiness for a controlled shadow migration rehearsal only.
It MUST NOT be interpreted as production readiness, full-corpus validation,
full-document semantic accuracy, or OCR accuracy.

## 9. Security/Dependency Status

retrieval, and deployment authorization until separately reviewed.

## 10. Migration-Readiness Decision Method

The evaluator MUST fail closed. Controlled rehearsal authorization requires:
D.5c `PASS`; D.5d `PASS` or `ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`; at least 3 evaluated
profiles; accepted records greater than 0; zero rejected records; zero unknown
provenance; zero collisions; determinism for all profiles; schema consistency;
no blocking issue codes; runtime ingestion unchanged; embeddings untouched;
Astra untouched; and FAISS untouched.

## 11. Record-Status Eligibility Policy

| Record status | Controlled rehearsal package | Controlled pilot | Production persistence | Production indexing/retrieval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `valid` | eligible | eligible | future approval required | future approval required |
| `valid_with_warnings` | eligible with approval | eligible with approval | blocked pending governance | blocked |
| `review_required` | quarantine only | blocked unless separately reviewed | blocked | forbidden |
| `rejected` | forbidden | forbidden | forbidden | forbidden |

`review_required` records MAY remain in an audit package, but they MUST NOT
enter an index, become retrieval-eligible, or be silently relabeled as valid.
Rejected records MUST remain visible in rejection reports.

## 12. Provenance Policy

| Provenance | Rehearsal | Production | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `full_provenance` | eligible | future approval required | eligible |
| `partial_provenance` | disabled by default | forbidden for indexing | quarantine |
| `legacy_filename_only` | legacy path only | blocked | quarantine |
| `unknown_provenance` | forbidden | forbidden | forbidden |

Partial provenance MAY be considered only in a future controlled pilot with
explicit per-record limitation, owner approvals, `review_required = true`, and
indexing/retrieval exclusion.

Personal names MUST NOT be committed in policy fixtures.

## 14. Limitation Ownership

Limitations MUST be scoped to a candidate, chunk, document, or explicitly
defined package. Candidate-level limitations MUST NOT become document-global
automatically. Document-level approvals MUST NOT become corpus-global
automatically. Absence of an approval record MUST fail closed.

## 15. Candidate-Table Governance

`TABLE_CANDIDATE_ONLY` MAY preserve candidate table text, but it MUST NOT prove
reconstructed row or cell accuracy. Table-specific answering SHOULD NOT claim
structured cell accuracy without later approval.

`TABLE_FALSE_POSITIVE_ON_FIGURE_PAGE` for the known page-52 candidate MUST
preserve the record, provenance, warning, limitation, and `review_required =
true`; it MUST remain quarantined from indexing and retrieval.

## 16. OCR Governance

OCR observations MUST be retained. Affected records MAY remain in a controlled
rehearsal package if identifiable by provenance, but they SHOULD be flagged or
quarantined. Production indexing MUST require OCR review or explicit page-level
exclusion. D.6 MUST NOT authorize OCR execution.

Controlled codes:

```text
OCR_REVIEW_REQUIRED
OCR_COMPLETENESS_NOT_ESTABLISHED
```

## 17. Legacy Coexistence

The next rehearsal MUST use shadow mode only. Structured records and legacy
chunks MUST retain separate origin metadata. Legacy chunks MUST NOT be deleted,
overwritten, silently merged, or cut over automatically.

## 18. Package Retention

Controlled rehearsal packages SHOULD be retained indefinitely under local
control until replacement validation succeeds. Production retention duration is
unresolved and MUST remain a condition before production persistence.

## 19. Replacement and Rollback

Replacement MUST generate a new package separately, validate it independently,
record exact source and policy identities, retain the previous package, record
digest comparison, record approval, and preserve rollback. In-place destructive
mutation MUST NOT occur.

## 20. Approval Records

Future approval records SHOULD include `approval_id`, `finding_code`,
`document_id`, `candidate_id`, `chunk_id`, `scope`, `owner_role`,
`reviewer_roles`, `disposition`, `rationale`, `authorized_phase`,
`indexing_eligible`, `retrieval_eligible`, `expiry_or_review_trigger`, and
`policy_version`. Approval records MUST NOT contain source text.

## 21. Security Gate

Dependency findings do not block local offline shadow rehearsal if no new
network exposure, deployment, production credentials, Astra operations, FAISS
operations, or dependencies are introduced. Production persistence, production
indexing, production retrieval, and deployment MUST require separate security
review.
